## Forks and redistribution

You are welcome to fork, study, modify and share the code. Before you
**distribute** a build of a fork (App Store, TestFlight, ad hoc, an `.ipa` or a
Mac app posted anywhere), you must:

1. **Change the name.** Do not call it PatchNode, nor anything that could be
   confused with it (for example "PatchNode Pro").
2. **Change the icon** and remove the Patchbay logotype and brand visuals.
3. **Change the bundle identifier** (`fr.antxis.apple.labwatch` and every
   identifier derived from it: widgets extension, App Group, iCloud container,
   background task identifiers).

You may say, accurately, that your app is "based on PatchNode" or "a fork of
PatchNode", with a link to this repository, as long as it is clear that your app
is not the official one and is not endorsed by the PatchNode project.

Building the app for yourself, on your own devices, does not require any of
this.
