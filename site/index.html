Your HomeLab at a glance

PatchNode keeps an eye on your self-hosted servers from your iPhone: Proxmox, Docker, alerts, backups, network, security and home automation. It talks directly to your servers, over your private network or VPN.

Supported: Proxmox VE, Proxmox Backup Server, Docker (Portainer or a read-only socket proxy), Prometheus, Alertmanager, Grafana, Uptime Kuma, Tailscale, CrowdSec, Nginx Proxy Manager, AdGuard Home, Home Assistant, Glances.

Consoles, SSH and Compose

Open Proxmox consoles, an SSH terminal to your own servers (key created on the device, fingerprints pinned) and manage Docker Compose projects, on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Private by design

  • No account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking.
  • Tokens stay in the iPhone's Keychain. Syncing your configuration, or your tokens through iCloud Keychain, is optional and off by default.
  • HTTPS only; actions always ask for confirmation and Face ID.

Try it without a server

The built-in demo shows a complete sample HomeLab, generated on the device.
